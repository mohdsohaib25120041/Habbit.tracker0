Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

GoalForce / Nexus ("the app") is a habit tracker and productivity application. Contact: support@goalforce.app.

What we collect

  • Without an account: everything you enter (habits, tasks, notes, water, settings) stays on your own device. We do not receive it.
  • With an account: your email address, and a backup copy of the data above so you can use the app on more than one device. We also record when you were last active, whether you have a Premium plan, and any feedback you choose to send.
  • Passwords are handled by our login provider and are never stored on your device or visible to us.

How we use it

To log you in, back up and sync your data, show your plan, send service messages (for example password reset), improve the app, and answer your feedback. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored securely with Supabase (database and login provider). Each user can only access their own data. Only the app administrators can see account details such as email, plan and activity, and they cannot read your notes or habit content through the admin panel.

Advertising and purchases (Android app)

The free version of the Android app may show ads from Google AdMob, which may use an advertising ID. Premium removes ads. Payments are handled by Google Play or official Premium codes; we do not see your card details.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete everything in the app: Settings → Account & Cloud Backup → Delete my account. This removes your account and your cloud backup.
  • Delete only the cloud backup: Settings → Delete cloud backup.
  • Data on your device: Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete everything, or clear the app's data in your device settings.
  • If you cannot use the app, email support@goalforce.app from your account email and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to stored data is restricted by database security rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
